Why Aivanta exists

Aivanta was founded by Alessandra Pocroianu, a second-year law student with a minor in Applied Physics.

Like many students, I use AI for a lot of things, and I’ve learned that without the right habits it can do more harm than good. AI was built as a tool to help us. When we don’t know how to use it well, it’s easy to end up following it instead of directing it.

As I get closer to graduation and look for internships and jobs, I keep meeting organizations that are afraid of AI. Some worry it will take over the work. Others worry it will strip out the human interaction their business depends on. That fear is reasonable. The answer is that AI shouldn’t replace people, and it shouldn’t work unsupervised.

I started Aivanta to show companies that AI isn’t the enemy, while keeping complete authority with real people. We help organizations use AI with clear rules, human review and a record of every decision. We’re starting with law firms, where accuracy and confidentiality matter most, and we’re building for any company or person who wants the same control.

Alessandra Pocroianu
Founder, Aivanta
